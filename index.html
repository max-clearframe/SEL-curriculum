001 / The Problem

Why AI
Literacy
Matters.

Widespread AI use reflects a fundamental shift in how we learn, think, and create. The implications for children are especially profound and largely unknown. This is the first generation growing up with tools that can talk back to them. How do we address students using AI for emotional support? Romantic engagement with chatbots? These are documented, real-world problems — and there is no playbook for them.

A new kind of curriculum for a new kind of challenge.
002 / The Curriculum

AI and
Digital
Life.

A social-emotional learning curriculum developed by Clearframe Learning that addresses these topics directly. Chapters like Parasocial AI Relationships and AI Psychosis examine emerging mental health impacts observed in users. Chapters like Who Controls This? and AI and Bias examine the inherent biases within large language models.
